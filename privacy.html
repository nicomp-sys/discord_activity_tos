Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 2026

1. Information Collection

We value your privacy. The App does not collect, store, transmit, or process any personal user data on external servers. We do not access your Discord account details, message content, or personal identifiers.

2. Local Session Storage

To ensure proper functioning and maintain your current state while using the App, temporary state data is stored exclusively within your client's browser local storage or session storage (`window.sessionStorage` / `window.localStorage`).

  • This data remains strictly on your local machine.
  • This data is never transmitted to our servers or third parties.
  • Closing your session or clearing your browser storage will remove this state.

3. Third-Party Services

The App operates within the Discord Embedded App SDK framework. While the App itself does not process personal data, your use of Discord is subject to Discord's Privacy Policy.

4. Contact Information

If you have any questions or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact the developer via the Discord application support channels.
